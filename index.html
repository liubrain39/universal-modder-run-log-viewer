What do these counts mean?
  • Lane 0 is baseline; lane 1 is homing. Shots come from turret totalShots.
  • Hits count UnitDamageEvent matches for that lane's bullet and designated target.
  • Simulation time stops when paused. An unfinished run is labeled incomplete.
  • Saved examples: two macOS runs reviewed on 2026-10-09. Other systems and multiplayer are untested.
  • Your own JSON is checked for format and valid counts; its origin is not verified.
